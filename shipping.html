Zero Shipping Charges

100% electronic SaaS & desktop software delivery. No physical courier or shipping charges apply (₹0).

1. Nature of Product & Mode of Delivery (100% Electronic)

AI Video Flow (operated by Anil Kumar Sharma / AI Video Flow Technologies) is a downloadable desktop software application (for macOS and Windows) and digital license/credit platform. Because all our products, subscriptions, and per-wedding credit packs are digital goods, no physical CD, DVD, USB drive, or printed box is shipped via postal or courier services.

2. Delivery Turnaround Time (Timeline)

  • Immediate On-Screen Delivery (0–60 Seconds): Immediately after your online payment (via UPI, Debit/Credit Card, NetBanking, or Wallet) is verified by our payment gateway partner, your unique cryptographic License Key (e.g., AIFLOW-PREM-XXXX-XXXX) and invoice are displayed on the payment confirmation page.
  • Automated Email & WhatsApp Delivery (Within 5 Minutes): A copy of your License Key, activation instructions, and payment receipt is automatically dispatched to the Email Address and WhatsApp mobile number entered during checkout.
  • Maximum Delivery Window (Up to 2 Hours): In rare instances where banking servers or payment gateway webhooks experience network delays, electronic delivery may take up to 2 hours from the time of payment deduction.

3. Shipping & Handling Fees

Since all software installers (.dmg for macOS and .exe for Windows) and activation keys are delivered electronically over the internet, Shipping and Delivery Charges are ₹0 (100% Free) across India and internationally.

4. How to Download & Activate Your Software

  • Step 1 — Download Installer: Visit https://aivideoflow.in/#download to download the latest AI Video Flow desktop installer for Windows 10/11 (64-bit) or macOS (Apple Silicon M1/M2/M3/M4 & Intel).
  • Step 2 — Enter License Key: Launch the AI Video Flow desktop app, open the License Activation panel, and paste the License Key received via screen/email/WhatsApp.
  • Step 3 — Self-Service Portal Access: You can also view your active license keys, remaining wedding credits, and invoice history anytime by logging into our Customer License Portal at https://license.aivideoflow.in/index.php/admin.
