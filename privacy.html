1. Introduction & Core Privacy Commitment

This Privacy Policy explains how AI Video Flow (operated by Anil Kumar Sharma / AI Video Flow Technologies, having its principal office in Siwan, Bihar - 841226, India) collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you visit https://aivideoflow.in, purchase licenses on https://license.aivideoflow.in, or use the AI Video Flow Desktop Application.

Zero Cloud Upload Guarantee: AI Video Flow operates as a native offline desktop software. Your clients' wedding footage, audio recordings, project timelines, and photos NEVER leave your local computer. We do not upload, view, store, or train external models on your private client footage.

2. Information We Collect

When you register, download our software, or purchase a subscription / wedding credit pack, we collect only the minimum information required to fulfill your order:

  • Customer Identity & Contact Data: Your Full Name, Studio/Business Name, Email Address, and WhatsApp Mobile Number (for instant license key delivery, invoices, and technical support).
  • Hardware Machine Fingerprint: An anonymized cryptographic hash of your computer hardware ID used solely to bind your license key to your authorized workstation and enable self-service machine lock resets.
  • Transaction & Billing Metadata: Order ID, Payment Transaction Reference, Plan selected, and billing state/country for tax and accounting compliance.

3. Payment Gateway & Financial Security

All online payments (UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, NetBanking, Wallets) are processed through RBI-authorized, PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment gateways (such as Razorpay / Cashfree / PhonePe). AI Video Flow does not collect, process, or store your sensitive banking credentials, card numbers, CVV, or UPI PINs on our servers.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate and deliver your software License Keys and wedding project credits via screen, email, and WhatsApp.
  • To verify license validity and credit balance when activating projects inside the desktop application.
  • To provide customer support, AnyDesk troubleshooting, and software update notifications.
  • To comply with applicable Indian tax, accounting, and legal obligations.

5. Cookies & Analytics

Our website uses essential session cookies required for checkout security, license portal authentication, and basic anonymous traffic diagnostics. You may disable non-essential cookies in your browser settings at any time without affecting your desktop software functionality.

6. Data Retention & Your Rights

We retain your license and transaction records for as long as your software license or wedding credits remain active, and as required by Indian tax laws. Under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal contact information by emailing us at support@aivideoflow.in.
