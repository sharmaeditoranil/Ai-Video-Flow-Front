Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Core Privacy Commitment: Local Footage Privacy

AI Video Flow operates primarily as an offline desktop software. Your clients' wedding footage, audio files, and photo media NEVER leave your local machine. We do not store, view, or stream your raw footage to any cloud server.

2. Information We Collect

When you purchase a license or wedding credits on https://aivideoflow.in or https://license.aivideoflow.in, we collect:

  • Name and Email Address (for license delivery and software updates).
  • WhatsApp Mobile Number (for instant license key delivery and technical assistance).
  • Anonymous Hardware Fingerprint (to lock your license safely to your computer and prevent unauthorized sharing).
  • Billing details processed securely via Razorpay (we never store your credit/debit card numbers or UPI PINs).

3. Payment Gateway Security

All online transactions are securely encrypted and processed by Razorpay, complying with PCI-DSS Level 1 standards. Your banking credentials remain completely private and secure.

4. Contacting Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team at support@aivideoflow.in or WhatsApp at +91 9939800780.
