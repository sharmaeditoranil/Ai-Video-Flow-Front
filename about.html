STUDIO GROUND TRUTH

Why Offline Processing Is Non-Negotiable

In Indian wedding cinematography, clients shoot on Sony FX3, FX6, Canon R5, and RED cinema cameras with 4K 10-bit 4:2:2 codecs. Uploading 200 GB to cloud servers is not practical for 99% of studios due to internet bandwidth limitations and strict client confidentiality.

That's why AI Video Flow runs 100% locally on your computer using Apple Silicon Neural Engine (M1-M4) and NVIDIA RTX hardware acceleration. Your raw wedding files never leave your SSD.

  • Absolute Data Privacy: No wedding footage ever leaves your Mac or PC.
  • Zero Internet Required: Work smoothly even in remote studio locations with zero connection.
  • Native Integration: 1-click export to DaVinci Resolve (.drp/.fcpxml), Premiere Pro XML, and EDIUS.
