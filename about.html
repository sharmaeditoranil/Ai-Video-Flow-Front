Why 100% Offline Processing Is Non-Negotiable

In professional wedding and commercial cinematography, studios shoot on Sony FX3, FX6, Canon R5, Lumix, and RED cinema cameras with heavy 4K 10-bit 4:2:2 codecs. Uploading 200 GB of raw footage to cloud servers is slow, expensive, and risks client privacy.

That is why AI Video Flow runs 100% locally on your workstation using Apple Silicon Neural Engine (M1–M4) and Windows GPU/CPU hardware acceleration. Your raw footage never leaves your local SSD.

  • Absolute Data Privacy: Raw 4K wedding & studio footage never leaves your Mac or PC.
  • Zero-Audio Multicam Sync: Frame-accurate alignment of 2–8 cameras & drones without scratch audio.
  • Native NLE Integration: 1-click timeline export to DaVinci Resolve, Adobe Premiere Pro, and EDIUS.
