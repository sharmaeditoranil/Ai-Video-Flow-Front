Pricing FAQ: Sabhi Jawaab Ek Jagah

Payment, activation aur credits ke baare me sabse zyada pooche jaane wale sawaal.

Kya wedding credits expire hote hain?
Bilkul nahi! Starter Pack, Studio Pack, aur Studio Pro Pack ke sabhi credits lifetime validity ke sath aate hain. Aap chahe to is lagan season me use karein ya agle saal — aapka ek bhi paisa waste nahi hota.
Payment hone ke baad key kab milti hai?
Turant! Razorpay par payment confirm hote hi success screen par aapki License Key AIFLOW-PREM-... display ho jaati hai aur sath hi aapke WhatsApp number aur email par auto-deliver ho jati hai.
Ek "Wedding Credit" ka kya matlab hai?
1 Wedding Credit ka matlab hai 1 complete wedding project ka timeline generation — jisme Highlight reel (3-5 min), Teaser (60s), aur Full Ceremony multicam assembly sab shamil hain chahe footage 50 GB ho ya 200 GB.
Agar computer format ho jaye ya naya PC khareedun to kya hoga?
Aapko naya pack khareedne ki zaroorat nahi hai. Hamare self-service Customer License Portal me jakar aap 1-click se apna Machine Lock reset kar sakte hain aur apne naye system par wahi key activate kar sakte hain.
