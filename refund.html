1. Free Trial Before Purchase

We strongly encourage all video editors and studios to download and test the Free Trial Edition of AI Video Flow before purchasing a paid subscription or wedding credit pack. This allows you to verify compatibility with your workstation hardware, camera codecs, and editing software (DaVinci Resolve, Adobe Premiere Pro, or Grass Valley EDIUS).

2. Order & Subscription Cancellation Policy

  • Subscription Cancellation (Monthly / Yearly Plans): You may cancel your recurring Creator, Pro, or Studio subscription at any time from your Customer License Portal or by messaging our support desk at least 24 hours before the next billing cycle. Upon cancellation, your license will remain active until the end of your current paid billing period, and no further charges will be made.
  • One-Time Wedding Credit Pack Cancellation: If you wish to cancel a newly purchased Per-Wedding Credit Pack (Starter, Studio, or Studio Pro Pack) before activating or consuming any credits, you may request a full cancellation and refund within 7 calendar days of purchase.

3. Refund Eligibility (7-Day Money-Back Guarantee)

You are eligible for a 100% Full Refund within 7 calendar days of your purchase under the following conditions:

  • You experience a critical software bug or NLE timeline bridge error on a supported Windows 10/11 or macOS system that our engineering support team is unable to resolve within 48 hours of your ticket/WhatsApp report; AND
  • You have consumed no more than one (1) wedding project credit (or exported no more than 1 full wedding project) from your purchased pack/plan; OR
  • You were charged twice (duplicate transaction) for the same order due to a payment gateway or UPI timeout.

4. Non-Refundable Scenarios

  • Refund requests submitted after 7 calendar days from the date of transaction.
  • Licenses where 2 or more wedding project credits have already been consumed and exported to DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro, or EDIUS.
  • Issues caused by unsupported operating systems (e.g., Windows 7/8 32-bit) or pirated/corrupted third-party NLE installations after refusal of remote AnyDesk diagnostic assistance.

5. Refund Processing Timeline & Method

Once your refund request is verified and approved by our billing team, the license key is deactivated and the refund is initiated immediately via our payment gateway partner (Razorpay / Cashfree / PhonePe). The refunded amount is automatically credited back to your original source of payment (UPI ID, Bank Account, Credit Card, or Debit Card) within 5 to 7 business days as per standard RBI banking timelines.
